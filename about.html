Alessia Obinu

/multidisciplinary artist/

Dancer • Choreographer • Visual Artist • Jewellery Designer

Born in Sardinia in 1997, currently based in Barcelona.

I am a professional contemporary dancer, choreographer, teacher, and painter.

Dance has accompanied me since childhood: I began dancing at the age of six. Since then, I have considered myself a deeply creative person, and all the experiences I have lived have shaped who I am today.

Creating is what I love to do. My work in dance and painting stems from an expressive urgency: the need to give form, through a physical, visual, and living language, to thoughts, emotions, and memories connected to the way we inhabit society.

Through imagination, I am able to shape what is intangible, making it perceptible. I am fascinated by the idea of translating invisible experiences, meditative states, spiritual dimensions, deep sensations, into the canvas as well as into the body. What belongs to the mind, what cannot be seen, through art can become presence; it can be shared and transformed into a sensitive and collective experience.

At the center of my research is the desire to ask questions, to open spaces for reflection, and to explore what the human experience implies. For me, art has the power to generate dialogue, to question, and to create connections.
