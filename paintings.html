A self-taught artist, she has always been deeply connected to art, a passion that has accompanied her since childhood.

Her first works took shape on simple white sheets of paper, using only a black pen. Guided by the desire to fill the space with lines and shapes that emerged from improvisation, she created without a precise outline—an instinctive and liberating gesture.

Over time, she felt the opposite need: to remove, to simplify, to leave space for emptiness. This led her to explore acrylic painting, a medium with which she began to reshape her visual language. Her current style leans toward surrealism, driven by the desire to convey something beyond the image and reality, exploring sensations, emotions, dreams, and other dimensions.

In January 2025, she held her first exhibition, BEYOND REALITY, at Sala Basiana in Barcelona. That same year, she was an artist in residence at Artquemy Gallery in Barcelona, an experience that added new depth to her artistic exploration, and she also took part in a collective exhibition at the same gallery. She was also one of the selected artists in the collective exhibition “DREAMS” by Arteaporte, in Turin, July 2026.

She is currently collaborating with Claudia Reig Martín’s Mythym project, creating illustrations for her book.
