/intro/ /paintings/

I am a self-taught artist, and I have always been deeply connected to art—a passion that has accompanied me since childhood.

My first works took shape on simple white sheets of paper, using only a black pen. Guided by the desire to fill the space with lines and shapes that emerged from improvisation, I created without a precise outline—an instinctive and liberating gesture.

Over time, I felt the opposite need: to remove, to simplify, and to leave space for emptiness. This led me to explore acrylic painting, a medium through which I began to reshape my visual language. My current style leans toward surrealism, driven by the desire to convey something beyond the image and reality, exploring sensations, emotions, dreams, and other dimensions.

In January 2025, I held my first exhibition, BEYOND REALITY, at Sala Basiana in Barcelona. That same year, I was an artist in residence at Artquemy Gallery in Barcelona, an experience that added new depth to my artistic exploration. I also took part in a collective exhibition at the same gallery. In July 2026, I was one of the selected artists for the collective exhibition “DREAMS” by Arteaporte in Turin.

I am currently collaborating with Claudia Reig Martín’s Mythym project, creating illustrations for her book.
